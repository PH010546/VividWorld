Vivid World changelog

v0.9.1
The chronicle (Ctrl+L) is fixed: it used to show only one entry, and names could appear as a string of code. Every entry now displays properly.
Names of people and places in the chronicle are now clickable and open their encyclopedia page.
The chronicle now shows the news you heard most recently at the top, and news you have heard stays there.
On long campaigns, the mod's disk and memory use no longer just keeps growing.
Existing campaigns can be continued as they are; no settings need to be changed.
