Vivid World changelog

v0.9.3
Fixed: a hero taken prisoner no longer keeps passing news on for the rest of that day.
Fixed a few rumor lines that said "someone" instead of naming the person.
New: integration with Calradia Remembers. With Calradia Remembers installed, a lord or wanderer you chat with through AI knows the rumors he has heard in Vivid World and still remembers, and can say who told him. Only Calradia Remembers is supported for now; other AI chat mods do not receive these rumors. A new "AI Integration" group in MCM sets the language used for this.
NPCs now say where a rumor came from, such as "I saw it myself" or "X told me that"; if you had heard someone was captured, news of his release comes as "Later, X told me that".
People involved in an event now tell it in their own voice, speaking as "I"; some embarrassing things they keep to themselves.
English rumor lines now use he or she according to the character's gender.
The "A confidence was let slip over the cups" and "A victory was belittled" news are removed for now and will return when secrets are reworked; ones you have already heard stay in the chronicle.
Existing campaigns can be continued as they are; no settings need to be changed.

v0.9.2
Passing on news heard from others no longer starts with "I was there, in fact"; only a participant or eyewitness says that now.
New rumor mode at the top of the "Dialogue" group in MCM: Auto, Casual or Realistic. Changes take effect immediately.
Casual: anyone who does not dislike you may bring up rumors, even early in a new game; close acquaintances tell the whole story, others only the gist. Commoners can ask too.
Realistic: a lord must think somewhat well of you before bringing up rumors; with mods such as Not-a-Noble or Lowborn, your clan needs tier 1 before you can ask.
Auto (default): Realistic when mods such as Not-a-Noble or Lowborn are detected, Casual otherwise. A window explains it the first time; after that, each load shows the current mode at the bottom left.
When someone has no news for you, they now say why: they would rather not say, have heard nothing lately, cannot recall, the news is out of date, or you have surely heard it already.
Existing campaigns can be continued as they are; no settings need to be changed. The rumor mode defaults to Auto and can be changed in MCM.

v0.9.1
The chronicle (Ctrl+L) is fixed: it used to show only one entry, and names could appear as a string of code. Every entry now displays properly.
Names of people and places in the chronicle are now clickable and open their encyclopedia page.
The chronicle now shows the news you heard most recently at the top, and news you have heard stays there.
On long campaigns, the mod's disk and memory use no longer just keeps growing.
Existing campaigns can be continued as they are; no settings need to be changed.
